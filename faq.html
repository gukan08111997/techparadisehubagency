Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about courses, fees and learning. Cannot find yours? Ask us.

Getting started

Do I need a technical background?

No. Excel, SQL and Python Basic start from zero. Each course page has a “Who it is for” line to help you pick where to begin.

Can I try a class before I enrol?

Yes. Book a free demo class from the contact page or the “Let's talk” button and we will confirm a slot by email.

Which course should I start with?

If you are new to data, start with Excel or Python Basic. If you want an analyst role, Data Analytics covers Excel, SQL and a dashboard tool. Tell us your goal and we will suggest a path.

Courses and crash courses

What is the difference between a course and a crash course?

A full course covers a subject in depth over several weeks. A crash course covers the essentials in five days and suits people with limited time or a deadline.

Can I take a crash course first and the full course later?

Yes. Many learners start with a crash course to test the subject, then continue. Ask us about the next batch dates.

Is there a project in every course?

Yes. Every course and crash course ends with a project you build yourself, so you leave with something to show.

Is the training online or in a classroom?

Ask us when you enquire and we will confirm the options available for the next batch.

Fees and enrolment

Where can I see the fees?

All course and crash course fees are on the Pricing page.

How do I enrol?

Send an enquiry or message us on WhatsApp. We will confirm the batch date and the payment steps, then reserve your seat.

Can I ask about payment options?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form and we will explain the options available.

Learning and support

Do I get a certificate?

You receive a course completion certificate after you submit your project.

What if I get stuck after class?

Send your question and you will get an answer by the next working day.

Do you guarantee a job?

No. We teach the skills and help you build a portfolio of projects. Hiring decisions belong to employers, and we do not promise placements.

Still have a question?

Message us and we will reply within one working day.
